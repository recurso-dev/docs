## What a dispute is

**Disputes** (in the left nav) is the queue of invoice queries your customers
raise from the [customer portal](/dashboard/customer-portal). Each dispute ties
a customer's objection to a specific invoice, and your team **reviews** it —
accepting it (optionally issuing a credit note in the same step) or rejecting it.

<Info>
Disputes here are **customer-raised invoice queries**, distinct from
payment-gateway chargebacks (card/ACH/bank-debit), which are handled
automatically with a ledger reversal. Accepting a dispute can optionally issue a
[credit note](/dashboard/credit-notes) for the disputed invoice; rejecting it
just closes the record.
</Info>

## The disputes list

The table shows one row per dispute:

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Invoice | The disputed invoice (short id) |
| Customer | Who raised it (short id) |
| Reason | The customer's stated reason |
| Status | **open** (amber), **resolved** (green), or **rejected** (red), with the note if any |
| Raised | When the dispute was created |

Use the **status filter** in the toolbar to switch between **Open**,
**Resolved**, **Rejected**, and **All**. The page opens filtered to **Open** so
the outstanding queue is front and center.

## Review a dispute

Open disputes show a **Review** button. The dialog shows the invoice and the
customer's reason, then lets you decide:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Optionally, tick “Issue a credit note on accept”">
    When accepting, this issues an **account credit** for the invoice's
    outstanding amount against the customer — using the same
    [credit note](/dashboard/credit-notes) flow (and ledger postings) as issuing
    one by hand. For a **partial** credit, leave the box unticked and create the
    credit note manually instead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a note (optional)">
    Record what was decided — the note is **visible to your team** and shows
    against the closed dispute.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Accept or Reject">
    **Accept** closes the dispute as **resolved** (and issues the credit if you
    ticked the box). **Reject** closes it as **rejected**. Either way it drops
    out of the open queue.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
The credit is issued **before** the dispute is marked resolved, so if credit
creation fails the dispute stays **open** for a clean retry — never a resolved
dispute with no credit behind it.
</Note>
