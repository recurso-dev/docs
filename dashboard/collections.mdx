## Overview

**Collections** (under Payments) is the operator view of payment recovery. The
automated engine — [smart dunning](/dashboard/dunning) retries, campaigns,
ACH settlement tracking — does the collecting; this page makes its state
legible and actionable: who is failing right now, why, what the machine will do
next, and the levers to intervene.

## The worklist

Every invoice currently in recovery appears in the table: `past_due` invoices
being chased and `uncollectible` invoices that were written off but still carry
a balance. Each row shows:

- **Customer** and invoice number
- **Amount** still owed (currency-aware)
- **Days overdue**, color-ramped as it ages
- **Last failure** — the gateway decline or ACH return code, humanized
  ("Insufficient funds", "ACH: account closed")
- **Next retry** — when the engine tries again, or a **paused** badge
- **Owner** — which engine is managing it: `scheduler` (email escalation),
  `worker` (smart retries), or `campaign` (multi-step sequences)
- ACH state chips: **settling** while a bank debit is in flight, **returned**
  after a bank claw-back reopened the invoice

Filter by status with the tabs; the list is paginated for large books.

## The funnel and failure reasons

Above the worklist:

- **Revenue at risk** — open money currently in dunning, FX-normalized to your
  reporting currency
- **Recovery rate** — of the cases *concluded in the last 90 days* (recovered
  or written off), the share recovered. A windowed cohort, so the number
  reflects how collection is performing *now*, not the whole history
- **Recovered (all-time)** and **Written off** totals
- **Top failure reasons** — decline codes ranked by how much revenue each is
  holding, so you can see whether the problem is expired cards, insufficient
  funds, or something systemic

<Info>
If some invoices are in a currency with no exchange rate available, the money
figures exclude them (never mis-summed) and the page shows an "amounts are
understated" note listing the affected currencies. Counts always include every
invoice.
</Info>

## Manual controls

Each row's action menu lets you intervene without fighting the automation:

### Retry now

Queues an immediate charge attempt through the smart-retry engine (picked up
within seconds). Guardrails refuse the retry — with a clear reason — when it
could double-charge:

- the invoice's dunning is **paused**,
- it's a **UPI AutoPay mandate** invoice (mandates can't be gateway-retried),
- an ACH debit is **still settling** (never stack a second charge on money in
  flight),
- or the invoice isn't actually past due anymore.

### Pause / resume dunning

Pausing stops *all* automated contact and charging for that invoice — retry
charges, escalation emails, and campaign steps (emails, SMS, payment walls)
alike. Use it while negotiating with a customer or investigating a dispute.
Resuming picks the schedule back up where it left off.

### Mark uncollectible

The manual write-off. The invoice stops being chased and moves to
`uncollectible` (a status change — you can still record a manual payment later
if the money ever arrives). The write-off date feeds the recovery-rate cohort.

## Best time to retry

The [Smart Dunning](/dashboard/dunning) page includes a **Best time to retry**
card: historical retry success rates by hour of day (UTC) and day of week, with
the best-performing windows highlighted once there's enough history to trust.
It's an insight into when your customers' payments succeed — the live retry
engine keeps learning independently.
