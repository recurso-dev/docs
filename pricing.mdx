Recurso's billing engine is **open source and free to self-host** — every feature, no
license fees, no usage caps, no lock-in. **Recurso Cloud** is managed hosting: free to
**$10,000 tracked revenue a month**, then **0.4% of volume or a flat $99** (whichever is
cheaper) — roughly half what Stripe Billing charges. **Enterprise** adds dedicated
infrastructure, compliance, and SLAs.

  Plan comparison, usage-based pricing, add-ons, and FAQ — on the Recurso site.
</Card>

## At a glance

| | Open Source | Cloud | Enterprise |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Price** | Free forever | Free to $10k/mo, then 0.4% or $99 | Custom |
| **Hosting** | Self-managed | Managed | Managed / on-prem |
| **Support** | Community | Email | Priority + SLA |
| **Best for** | Teams with DevOps who want full control | Shipping billing without managing infra | High volume, compliance, custom needs |

<Info>
  Self-hosting includes **everything** — the advanced capabilities that are paid add-ons
  on Cloud (advanced analytics, accounting sync, multi-currency FX) are free in the
  open-source edition, because you run the infrastructure.
</Info>
